# EICRecon handoff

The producer reads persisted cluster energy/xyz, hit cellID/energy/xyz, cluster-to-hit ObjectIDs and **assigned hit-energy contributions**, plus event identity and reconstructed primary vertex (or a configured nominal IP). Neutral selection requires track-match ObjectIDs. MC and cluster/MC associations are optional diagnostics. Collection names are configurable; relation targets/offsets, exact cellIDs, energy assignments and cross-shard positions are checked. Cache declarations must match geometry, cellID encoding and baseline calibration. No EICRecon installation or rebuild is required to derive gains.

For original contributions c and shower energy E0, `E(G)=E0*sum(G*c)/sum(c)` keeps the original response factor and directions fixed. The Jacobian is `Jik=m_i²*(c1ik/current_sum1+c2ik/current_sum2)` with current gain-weighted contributions, including shared cells. Inactive channels remain in the denominators. `r=m²-m0²`, `C=J.T@J`, `D=-J.T@r`, `L=J.T@1`, `B=sum(r)`. The truncated solve uses `lambda=(B+L.T Cplus D)/(L.T Cplus L)` and `epsilon=Cplus(D-lambda L)`, followed by positive multiplicative updates with the existing step cap and L1-constraint merit/backtracking. No smoothing/prior or generator weight enters this unweighted objective.

The output `relative_gain` is a multiplier on the stated existing hit-energy baseline, not an absolute electronics calibration. Where hit reconstruction divides by baseline factor f0, multiplying energy by G requires **f0/G**. The historical demonstration's f0=0.96 was configuration-specific.

The project's existing `replay.py`/`run_replay.py` demonstrated a bounded 30-event fixed raw-hit replay using a samplingFraction expression over decoded cellID fields in EICRecon revision 729b967b0258383f36aaa95fd745df6ab86c337e. Stored RecHits/clusters were omitted so official hit reconstruction, island clustering and CoG recomputed downstream objects. Unity reproduced 275 hits and cluster energies; a +10% multiplier on cellID33640807 changed its hit by 10% and its affected cluster from 2.577463865 to 2.754847288 GeV. This was an intermediate clustering check, not complete PID/shape reconstruction or validation of a 2740-channel map. Earlier ADC suppression cannot be undone by later gains.

The intended production interface is a **native cellID-keyed conditions/lookup payload** with geometry/encoding/baseline/version validity and explicit review policy. This package does not implement that service or claim EICRecon reads its CSV natively. A tiny decoded-field expression is evidence for the convention, not a scalable conditions system.

The three stages remain distinct: (1) fitting with fixed showers, delivered here; (2) applying approved gains before official clustering, demonstrated only in the bounded prior replay; (3) repeating reconstruction and calibration with updated memberships/directions and detector QA, still an integration task. Detector/software owners must approve constants and specify fallback for unsupported/flagged channels. No filtered application map is produced automatically. Keep previous baseline constants where approval policy rejects a candidate.

Machine interfaces are documented in `schemas/` and `docs/configuration.md`. First integration work: agree the cellID payload and baseline version, add native lookup in the appropriate reconstruction configuration, force downstream reconstruction from fixed raw input, then perform bounded unity/nonunity and physics closure checks before any full production replay.
